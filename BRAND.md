# SnapFit — Brand

## Name
**SnapFit**

Tagline options:
- Resize & compress for social — private & offline
- Social-ready photos in one click. Your images never upload.
- Snap to the right size for Instagram, X, LinkedIn & more.

## Positioning
A simple, friendly **browser tool** that resizes and compresses photos for common social sizes. Mass market creators, small businesses, and anyone posting online — not designers with Photoshop, not enterprises.

**Price:** $2.99 lifetime unlock (one-time). Free tier with light limits so people can try before buying.

**Privacy hook:** Processing is 100% client-side. “Never uploads your photos.” Works as a static site / offline after download.

## Audience
- Creators & side-hustlers posting to Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook
- Small business owners who need “make this fit the post size” without learning design tools
- Anyone who hates cloud uploaders that eat privacy or require accounts

**Not for:** Print shops, pro photo studios, agencies needing DAM, or people seeking AI generation / background removal as the core product.

## Voice
- Warm, clear, a little playful (“snap” = quick ready-for-feed crop)
- Short sentences. Benefit-first. No enterprise jargon.
- Honest about free limits; proud of privacy and the one-time price
- Never guilt-trip or dark-pattern the unlock

Examples:
- ✅ “Drop a photo. Pick Instagram Story. Download. Done — and it never left your laptop.”
- ✅ “$2.99 once. No subscription. No uploads.”
- ❌ “Leverage our synergistic media optimization pipeline…”

## Visual vibe
- Dark, vibrant gradients (violet → pink, teal accents) — modern consumer app, not SaaS gray
- Soft panels, rounded pills, friendly emoji-light UI
- Feels like a small indie product you’d buy on Gumroad, not a Fortune 500 admin console

## What we never sell (as SnapFit)
- Freelancer proposal / invoice PDF packs
- DeskRun or AI operator / “hire an agent” services
- Green Desk (or other desk/office brands)
- Subscriptions, stock photo marketplaces, or “upload to our cloud” as the product
- Anything that requires paid third-party APIs for the core resize/compress loop

## Naming note
This product was previously developed under a different working name. Public marketing and UI use **SnapFit only**.

## Promise
Fast social-ready images. Private by design. Cheap forever unlock.
